Xinwei Ma

Associate Professor of Economics
University of California San Diego

Xinwei Ma is an Associate Professor of Economics at the University of California San Diego. His interdisciplinary research focuses on developing robust statistical methods for causal inference and program evaluation in economics, the social sciences, and biomedical applications.

Xinwei earned his Ph.D. in Economics from the University of Michigan in 2019. He previously received a Master of Finance from the University of Hong Kong and a Master of Economics and a Bachelor of Science from Peking University. He serves as an Associate Editor for Econometric Theory, the Journal of Econometrics, and The Econometrics Journal.
